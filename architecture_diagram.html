1. Intake & Discovery

Uses Exa API to find verified speaker credentials and talking points, and DuckDuckGo to analyze active competitor angles, storing verified claims in Neo4j.

Say this: "You just enter a speaker's name or goal—the AI immediately researches their credentials and market trends automatically."
Exa API · DuckDuckGo · Neo4j

2. Strategic Planning (LangGraph)

5 specialist agents run in parallel and merge into the Chief Strategist. Marketers can refine the plan iteratively using the Plan Refinement Agent (Revise · Apply · Reply).

Say this: "Rather than one generic prompt, five specialized agents debate and draft the strategy, with Human-in-the-Loop review."
LangGraph · 5-Specialist Fan-Out · HITL

3. Creative Studio (Posts + Video)

Drafts LinkedIn posts evaluated by 3 subagents (Hooks, Hashtags, Readability). Simultaneously, the Video Reel Studio generates scene images (Pollinations), voiceover (Edge-TTS), and stitches 9:16 vertical video reels (MoviePy).

Say this: "It writes polished LinkedIn copy and automatically renders short-form 9:16 video reels with voiceovers and subtitles."
MoviePy · Pollinations · Edge-TTS · Gemini

4. Distribution & Supabase

Every post passes through a Brand Safety Validator. The LinkedIn AutoPilot schedules publishing via Unipile, while Supabase persists campaign data and 9:16 video MP4s.

Say this: "It safely schedules, publishes, and engages with your audience on LinkedIn with automated guardrails."
Supabase · Unipile · Circuit Breaker
