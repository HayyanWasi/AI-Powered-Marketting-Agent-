# AI Event Marketing Automation System

## Objective

Build an AI marketing system that increases awareness, engagement,
registrations, attendance, and long-term community growth for events.

## Workflow

``` text
Organization Knowledge
        ↓
Guest Research
        ↓
Audience Research
        ↓
Marketing Strategy
        ↓
Campaign Planning
        ↓
Content Planning
        ↓
Content Generation
        ↓
Scheduling
        ↓
Publishing
        ↓
Analytics
        ↓
Optimization
```

## Agents

### 1. Organization Knowledge Agent

Store: - Mission, vision, story - Brand tone - Founder/team - Services -
Previous events - Sponsors - Testimonials - Social links

Event data: - Name - Theme - Goal - Date/time - Venue - Registration
link - Agenda - FAQ

### 2. Guest Research Agent

Collect: - Name, photo, bio - Designation/company - Achievements -
Expertise - Speaking topics - Previous talks - Quotes - Social
profiles - Images/videos

### 3. Audience Research Agent

Collect: - Personas - Goals - Pain points - Interests - Platforms -
Preferred content - Best posting times

### 4. Marketing Strategy Agent

Decide: - Desired audience perception - Campaign goals - Messaging -
CTA - KPIs

### 5. Campaign Planner

Campaigns: - Event announcement - Meet the guest - Organization story -
Educational series - Behind the scenes - Testimonials - Countdown - Live
coverage - Post-event highlights

### 6. Content Planner

Content pillars: - Organization - Guests - Event - Educational -
Community - Social proof - Urgency - Behind the scenes

### 7. Content Generation Agent

Inputs: - Organization - Guest - Campaign - Audience - Platform - Tone -
Goal - CTA

Outputs: - LinkedIn - Instagram - Facebook - X - Email - WhatsApp -
Blog - Reel scripts - Carousel copy - Image/video prompts

### 8. Scheduler

Publish according to campaign timeline.

### 9. Analytics Agent

Track: - Reach - Impressions - Engagement - CTR - Registrations -
Attendance - Best guests - Best content - Best platform - Best posting
time

### 10. Optimization Agent

Improve future campaigns using analytics.

# Campaign Timeline

## 45--30 Days Before

Goal: Awareness - Event announcement - Theme reveal - Organizer
introduction - Registration opens - Previous event highlights -
Testimonials

## 30--21 Days

Goal: Authority - Guest introductions - Guest achievements -
Organization achievements - Sponsor announcements - Agenda preview

## 21--14 Days

Goal: Interest - Speaker topics - Educational posts - Behind the
scenes - Polls - Q&A

## 14--7 Days

Goal: Engagement - Countdown - More guests - Venue preparation - Team
content - Community questions

## 7--3 Days

Goal: Urgency - Daily countdown - Venue preview - Final agenda -
Registration reminders - Stories throughout the day

## 2--1 Days

Goal: Final excitement - Stage setup - Venue ready - Welcome message -
Parking/location - Checklist - Final reminder

# Event Day Schedule

  Time               Action                 Goal
  ------------------ ---------------------- ---------------
  Morning            Today's the day        Excitement
  Venue opens        Venue photos           Trust
  Registration       Arrival photos         Social proof
  Opening            Live photos/video      Engagement
  Every speaker      Speaker intro          Promote guest
  During sessions    Quotes                 Education
  Every 20--30 min   Audience/BTS updates   Engagement
  Networking         Networking photos      Community
  Sponsors           Sponsor highlights     Sponsor value
  Closing            Group photo            Community
  End                Thank you              Retention

## Live Content Rules

When guest arrives: - Welcome post - Arrival story - Introduction

When keynote starts: - Speaker intro - Stage photo - Session topic

Every 20--30 minutes: Check for: - Audience engagement - Full hall -
Networking - Great photos - Memorable moments

If found: - Story - Reel - LinkedIn update

If memorable quote: - Quote graphic - Multi-platform post

If networking: - Community photos - Connection highlights

# Post Event

-   Speaker appreciation
-   Volunteer appreciation
-   Sponsor appreciation

## Next 7 Days

-   Photo album
-   Highlight video
-   Session summaries

-   Event statistics
-   Next event teaser

# Follow-up Automation

Before registration: - Reminder after 24h - Value reminder after 3
days - Deadline reminder after 7 days

After registration: - Confirmation - 7-day reminder - 3-day reminder -
1-day reminder - Event-day reminder

After event: - Thank you - Feedback request - Share media - Invite to
community - Promote future events
