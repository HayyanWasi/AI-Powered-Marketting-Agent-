# AI Marketing Operating System Plan

## Goal
Build a modular marketing automation system for events, guest promotion, and organization branding that can research, plan, generate, schedule, and optimize campaigns.

## Core Principle
Do not start with content generation. Start with data, strategy, and audience understanding. Content should be the final output of a structured workflow.

## Full Workflow
1. Organization knowledge collection.
2. Guest research collection.
3. Audience research collection.
4. Marketing strategy selection.
5. Campaign planning.
6. Content planning.
7. Content generation.
8. Asset generation.
9. Scheduling and publishing.
10. Performance analytics.
11. Optimization.

- Organization Knowledge Agent.

## 8. Posting Schedule
Use the event timeline to decide posting frequency.

### 45 to 30 days before event
Goal: Awareness.
- 1 post per day.
- Introduce event.
- Introduce guest.
- Share event purpose.
- Share organization story.

### 30 to 15 days before event
Goal: Authority.
- 1 post per day.
- Share guest achievements.
- Share educational content.
- Share FAQs.
- Share speaker value.

### 15 to 7 days before event
Goal: Engagement.
- 1 to 2 posts per day.
- Polls.
- Reels.
- Quizzes.
- BTS content.
- Testimonials.
- Questions and answers.

### 7 to 1 day before event
Goal: Urgency.
- 2 to 3 posts per day.
- Countdown.
- Last call.
- Guest quotes.
- Venue reminders.
- Registration reminder.

### Event day
Goal: Live engagement.
- Live photos.
- Live reels.
- Live stories.
- Guest arrivals.
- Key moments.
- Audience reactions.
- Speaker highlights.

### 7 days after event
Goal: Retention.
- Thank-you post.
- Event recap.
- Photo album.
- Video highlights.
- Testimonials.
- Survey.
- Future event invitation.

## 9. Follow-up Automation
Follow-ups are one of the highest-value parts of event marketing.

### Before registration
- 24 hours after visit: reminder with value.
- 3 days later: featured guest or session highlight.
- 7 days later: deadline or scarcity message only if true.

### After registration
- Immediate confirmation.
- 7 days before: preparation tips.
- 3 days before: guest and agenda highlights.
- 1 day before: venue, timing, parking, checklist.
- Event morning: welcome and check-in reminder.

### After event
- Within 24 hours: thank-you message.
- When available: photos and recordings.
- Feedback request.
- Community or newsletter invitation.
- Future event promotion.

## Audience Persuasion Rules
Use ethical persuasion, not manipulation.

### What to focus on
- Relevance.
- Authority.
- Social proof.
- Belonging.
- Convenience.
- Real urgency.
- Clear value.

### Questions every message should answer
- Why should I care?
- What will I gain?
- Can I trust this?
- Who else is attending?
- What do I lose if I ignore this?
- Is it easy to join?

## Final System Flow
Organization Database -> Guest Research -> Audience Research -> Strategy -> Campaign Planning -> Content Planning -> Content Generation -> Asset Generation -> Scheduling -> Publishing -> Analytics -> Optimization.

## Final Recommendation
Start with one event type and one guest workflow. Keep the system simple at first, then expand into more guest profiles, more platforms, and deeper automation.
